# Contextual anti-patterns

## Always avoid

- generic template structure with no product-specific idea;
- indiscriminate card grids;
- random design-style mixing;
- fake social proof, metrics, or customer logos;
- inaccessible contrast;
- emoji as structural icons;
- placeholder content presented as final;
- no loading/error/empty states;
- production hotlinks to random images;
- untested responsive layouts;
- animation without reduced-motion support.

## Not universally bad

Gradients, glass, large type, brutalism, maximalism, 3D, dark mode, glow,
asymmetry, strong shadow, and unconventional navigation can be excellent.
Use them only when they belong to the selected direction and are executed coherently.
