# Depth, effects, and surface language

Choose one coherent depth model:

- flat graphic;
- bordered editorial;
- softly elevated;
- layered glass/translucent;
- high-contrast dimensional;
- tactile or skeuomorphic.

Use effects consistently. Define:

- surface ladder;
- border contrast;
- radius scale;
- shadow scale;
- blur/transparency rules;
- texture and gradient rules.

Glass, glow, gradients, grain, and strong shadows can be excellent when they
support the concept. They become low quality when used as uncoordinated decoration.
