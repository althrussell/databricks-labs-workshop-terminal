# Forms and product states

## Forms

- Use visible labels.
- Group related fields and progressively disclose advanced options.
- Place errors near the field and explain recovery.
- Preserve user input after validation errors.
- Show disabled, busy, success, and destructive states clearly.

## Required states

For data or networked surfaces, design:

- initial/loading;
- empty/new user;
- partial/stale;
- error/retry;
- success/confirmation;
- permission denied;
- offline/degraded when relevant;
- long text and large result sets.
