# Critique and refinement

## Pass A: concept and composition

Score 1–5:

- distinctiveness;
- task/message clarity;
- hierarchy;
- brand coherence;
- composition fit;
- signature moment.

Fix scores below 4 before detail polish.

## Pass B: craft and interaction

Score 1–5:

- spacing and alignment;
- typography;
- colour/contrast;
- icon and asset consistency;
- interaction feedback;
- responsive behaviour;
- states and accessibility;
- performance/loading quality.

Record what changed after each pass. Do not merely state that the design was reviewed.
