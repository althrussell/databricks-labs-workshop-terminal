# Creative direction

A direction is a coherent product idea, not a palette.

Each direction must define:

- **Concept:** the single visual metaphor or principle.
- **Audience emotion:** confidence, curiosity, calm, urgency, delight, trust, prestige, etc.
- **Composition:** editorial, immersive, modular, spatial, dense, narrative, or transactional.
- **Type character:** geometric, humanist, grotesk, serif editorial, mono-accented, playful, etc.
- **Colour logic:** restrained neutral, saturated brand, tonal monochrome, complementary contrast, etc.
- **Imagery:** documentary photography, product macro, technical diagrams, abstract geometry, illustration.
- **Motion:** restrained feedback, cinematic reveal, spatial transitions, data motion.
- **Signature moment:** one memorable interaction or composition.

Three directions should be meaningfully different. Do not show the same wireframe
with three colour themes.
