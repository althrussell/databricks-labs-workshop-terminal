# Product and page archetypes

Choose by user task, not framework.

## Product archetypes

- Branded marketing or campaign site
- SaaS or AI product
- Internal operations tool
- Analytical or data product
- Ecommerce or catalogue
- Marketplace
- Portfolio or editorial publication
- Education or guided learning
- Event, community, or membership experience
- Healthcare or high-trust service
- Finance or regulated workflow
- Developer tool
- Creative tool
- Consumer utility

## Page archetypes

- Hero-led narrative
- Editorial landing page
- Product demonstration
- Conversion page
- Repository/explorer
- Operations console
- Investigation workbench
- Workflow/form sequence
- Dashboard/analytical overview
- Feed or catalogue
- Detail/configurator
- Conversational/agent workspace

Never default every app to a dashboard or every website to a centred hero plus six cards.
