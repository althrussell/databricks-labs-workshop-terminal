# Brand and art direction

## Existing brand

Extract and respect:

- logo/wordmark rules;
- primary, secondary, and neutral colours;
- typography and fallback stacks;
- iconography and illustration conventions;
- photography treatment;
- border, radius, texture, and elevation language;
- tone of voice and vocabulary.

## No existing brand

Create a compact system, not a random logo:

- one-sentence brand promise;
- 3–5 colour roles;
- display and body type choices;
- icon family;
- imagery direction;
- shape language;
- motion personality;
- microcopy tone.

Brand systems should be memorable because of consistency and point of view, not
because every visual technique is used at once.
