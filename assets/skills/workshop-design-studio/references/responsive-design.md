# Responsive design

Validate at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440px unless the product has different targets.

- Convert multi-column layouts into meaningful sequences.
- Preserve the primary action and context.
- Avoid horizontal overflow.
- Use fluid type and spacing carefully; do not let headings dominate small screens.
- Adapt navigation rather than compressing it beyond usability.
- Make dense tables scroll, collapse, or expose focused detail intentionally.
- Verify sticky and fixed elements do not cover content.
